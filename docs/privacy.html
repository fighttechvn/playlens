PlayLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Short version: PlayLens collects nothing. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Everything happens inside your browser.

What the extension does

PlayLens runs only on play.google.com pages. To display download counts, review counts and last-updated dates, it fetches each app's public Google Play detail page directly from play.google.com — the same pages you could open yourself. These requests go straight from your browser to Google; they never pass through any server operated by us.

Data stored on your device

  • Settings (feature-flag toggles) are stored in chrome.storage.sync so they follow your Chrome profile.
  • A cache of public app data (downloads / reviews / update date per app) is stored in chrome.storage.local for up to 12 hours, so pages load faster. You can clear it anytime from the extension's settings page.

Neither store contains any personal information, browsing history, or identifiers.

Data collected or shared

None. PlayLens does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data with anyone. It contains no analytics, telemetry, ads, or third-party code.

Permissions explained

  • storage — save your settings and the 12-hour cache described above.
  • clipboardWrite — used only when you click the CSV button, to copy the app list to your clipboard.
  • Access to play.google.com — required to read list pages and fetch public app detail pages. The extension never runs on any other site.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will be published at this address and noted in the project's release notes.

Contact

PlayLens is open source. Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/fighttechvn/playlens/issues.
