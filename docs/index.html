PlayLens
See every app's stats, right on the list.

Free open-source Chrome extension that shows download counts, review counts and last-updated dates for every app directly on Google Play list pages — developer pages, search results, collections. No more opening apps one by one.

⬇ Download for Chrome ★ View on GitHub

MIT licensed · no tracking · all data stays in your browser

Three views, your choice

🏷️

Icon overlay badge

A compact strip on the bottom of each app icon — installs, rating × review count and update date. Zero layout shift, and it steps aside automatically when the inline line is on.

📝

Inline under the rating

An extra info line right below each card's own rating, styled to match Play. Update dates are color-coded: green ≤ 6 months, amber ≤ 18, red older.

📊

Sortable side panel

A table of every scanned app — sort by downloads, rating, reviews or update date; click a row to open the app; export the list as CSV.

⚙️

Feature flags

Every view can be toggled independently from the popup or the full settings page. Changes apply instantly — no page reload.

🎯

Exact review counts

Data is parsed from each app's own detail page (JSON-LD), so review counts are exact — 824.1K, not "824K-ish".

🔒

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no external servers. Fetches go straight to play.google.com and results are cached 12h locally.

Install in 30 seconds

  1. Download playlens.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Open any Google Play list page — stats appear on every app card.

Questions

How do I see the download count of an app without opening it?

Install PlayLens and open any Google Play list page. The download count, rating, review count and last-updated date appear on each app card directly — no clicking through.

Which Google Play pages does it work on?

Any page that shows a grid or row of app cards: developer pages, search results, category and collection pages, "Similar apps", and the Play home page.

Are the review counts exact?

Yes. Play's own cards round to something like "824K". PlayLens reads the structured data on each app's detail page, so you get 824.1K — the real figure, useful when you are comparing two apps that both round to the same number.

Can I export the list to a spreadsheet?

Yes. Open the side panel and click CSV — the whole scanned list is copied to your clipboard, ready to paste into Sheets or Excel.

Does it collect or send my data anywhere?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no server of ours. PlayLens runs only on play.google.com, fetches the same public pages you could open yourself, and caches results on your device for 12 hours. See the privacy policy.

Does it work in Edge, Brave or Opera?

Yes — it is a standard Manifest V3 extension, so any Chromium-based browser can load it.
